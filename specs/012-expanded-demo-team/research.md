# Research
Current demo_template chooses existing full lawyers or admin; seed_operations adds six scoped identities excluded from /admin/lawyers. Thus a larger scoped roster alone would not fill the screenshot chart. Decision: dedicated 12 sample identities, scoped sample-case allowlist and explicit admin output metadata. Alternatives rejected: unrestricted synthetic users (would have customer data access), changing existing users (out of scope), display-only fake chart rows (no real assignment workflow). Existing sample grants and six workflow personas remain unchanged. Initial distribution audit ensures repeat import preserves manual assignment.
